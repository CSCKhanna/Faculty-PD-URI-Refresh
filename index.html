Button Guide

Filters

Search narrows by title, provider, topic, audience, description, access note, or source.

Sort orders by date, provider, or priority.

Timing limits the list to all year, upcoming, summer, fall, or spring.

Views and Exports

Cards is the full browsing view and moves the card results to the top of the viewport.

Timeline groups items by month and moves the timeline results to the top of the viewport.

Table is a compact list for scanning and moves the table results to the top of the viewport.

CSV, Calendar, Announcement, and Print export the current filtered list.

Audience

Audience chips show opportunities for selected career stages, appointment types, and leadership roles.

Select more than one audience to combine their matching opportunities.

Click an active audience chip again to remove it from the filter.

Provider and Topic Chips

Provider chips show only opportunities from selected organizations or categories such as Other.

Topic chips show selected themes such as AI, Writing, Promotion & Tenure, Teaching, or Mentoring. Career stages are grouped under Audience.

Source links open the provider or evidence page for a card or access note.
